How HealthMemory works

A simple guide to your health organiser.

1. Create profiles

Add yourself and family members and select the active profile.

2. Store medical records

Upload PDF reports or images with dates and notes.

3. Manage medicines

Set medicine details, schedule and duration. Mark doses done daily.

4. Schedule appointments

Save doctor details, speciality, date, time and clinic.

5. Review timeline

See medical records, medicines and appointments chronologically.

6. Backup

Download a JSON backup regularly and restore it when needed.

HealthMemory is an organiser, not a medical service. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional advice.
Demo privacy: Information is stored locally in your browser. This is not encrypted medical storage.
